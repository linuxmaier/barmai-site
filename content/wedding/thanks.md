We're really grateful for all the ways that people contributed to making our wedding happen. When we started planning our wedding, one of our goals with it was to make it a way for us to experience the love and support of the people in our lives. The ways that people contributed their time, their thoughts, their resources, and their well wishes and comforts just really touched us, and we wanted to make sure we had a place we could say thank you properly.

## Officiant

- **Maddie**: We were so glad you were willing to be our officiant and help us create a ceremony that brought us together in a very *us* way. You were patient, creative, helpful, and game to be yourself. Thanks for being our sibling <3

## Music

- **Corey**: You were so generous with your time and expertise! Music is so important to us and our relationship, and we're grateful to you for helping us thread music into our wedding.

## Decoration

- **Kate**: We had a lot to do and not very much time to do it in, and we seriously don't know how we would have gotten everything ironed out if it wasn't for your help. In so many ways your aesthetic and creative sense is imprinted all over our wedding, from the desk to the flowers to the place settings to the bookmarks. Taking a feeling and bringing it into the world as something that can be seen and touched is very hard, and we're so happy to have gotten to benefit from the lifetime of care and craft you've put into that work. Thank you.

## Photography
