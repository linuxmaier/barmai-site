The pitch for this book is weird: it's about a man who discovers that he has a medical condition where his body slowly turns into a great white shark. I didn't really understand the appeal when my Dad & Jane first recommended it to us, but Hanna picked it up. And then later, as they finished the book sobbing in their hammock, they insisted that I needed to read it so they could talk about it with me. So then *I* read it and holy smokes, is this a moving book. The exploration of what it means to know that the end of your life as you know it is coming is so much more grounded than the outlandish premise would imply, evoking death, dementia, divorce, and the transformative rebirth that exists for us and our loved ones on the other side of all of those scary transitions. Hanna picked a better place to finish the book; I had to deal with fighting back tears on an airplane.

— Andrew
