I read this book multiple times in 2nd grade before I found another book I was willing to read. I can't say what drew me to it, but if I had to guess, I may have seen myself in Matilda's thirst for knowledge. And while my family was nothing like the Wormwoods, the family Matilda built with Miss Honey was no less beautiful or aspirational.

— Hanna
