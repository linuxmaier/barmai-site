When I tell you that what I remember most from these books is the food, I expect that there are going to be two reactions. The folks who haven't read Redwall are going to be confused, and the folks who have are going to be nodding vigorously. I read these books fairly young and I didn't carry away from them any serious thoughts or deep-set values, but it's a remarkable act of conjuring for Brian Jacques to have drawn a picky eater like me into fantasizing about strawberry cordial, cornbread with hazelnuts, and deeper 'n ever pie. I'm used to writers being able to make me fear, hope, love, or laugh. I marvel at an author who can make my mouth water without using any pictures. It's like magic.

— Andrew
