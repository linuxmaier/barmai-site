Unlike Ursula's other books this is a relatively straight forward read (not too many names and places to make sense of); however, consistent with her other works, Lathe of Heaven cuts straight to the heart of being human. The protagonist, George Orr, discovers that his dreams alter reality—past and future—and when he seeks help from a psychiatrist, who is primarily well-intentioned in his desire to control George's dreams, crazy things start happening. It's a quick read with many twists and turns (I recommend not reading any spoilers before you pick this one up) and thought provoking consequences.

— Hanna
