Hanna and I read this as part of our slow-running, two-person parenting book club. I'm not sure where they'd heard of it but I do remember they were the one to recommend it, and to my shock it's now one of my top five favorite books. Not favorite parenting books, but just favorite overall. I feel like I'm kind of picky when someone's trying to give me advice, and if it feels like there's a real disconnect between how the author and I relate to the world, it's hard to adopt the suggestion. This book felt so grounded in a curious, open-minded willingness to find the world as it is, rather than as we want it to be, and that same clear eyed openmindedness about how we parents impact our children really moved me. So many of the anxieties I could feel about how my kid will turn out kind of melt away into a focus on what I actually want to get out of being a parent. It's an opportunity to turn stress into joy, and I'm grateful to this book (and Hanna for the recommendation) for giving that to me.

— Andrew
