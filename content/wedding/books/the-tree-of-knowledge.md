I'm gonna be honest: I haven't finished this book. I have tried twice, and both times I found it such dense work that I had to set it down. Not because it's impossible to understand, but just because it felt like each section sent my mind off running, thinking about the implications of the world the authors describe. I believe, fundamentally, that the order that we see in the world is something our minds put there, rather than some kind of cosmic intention. The creation of things like logic and law and culture and art is a side effect of a chaotic process of evolution that has no author and no direction. A happy accident. But that's not very satisfying, because the way we live is right inside that order, that intention. Reading this book makes me feel like I'm nibbling around the edges of a very big thought that helps me link those two different beliefs into one vibrant understanding of the world we live in, and what it means for us to think.


— Andrew
