When my friend Madison gifted me this book I felt very seen—and that was only more true after reading it. Sabrina Imbler's book is a collection of poetic essays reflecting on their life and relationships and the wisdom that can be found in ocean creatures. Reading each essay, like scuba diving, was an immersion that changed me. I'm particularly fond of the chapter about Salps, where Sabrina shares a vision for queer community on the shores of Riis beach. One that welcomes and includes the heartbreak of past love: "I want to see all of my exes—let them come—and to feel a surge of remembrance of the times I loved them most, our moments of greatest connection, the closest we came to imagining a future together."

— Hanna
