As Hanna and Andrew prepared their vows, they realized that what they wanted to promise each other was not a list of things to do for as long as they are together. Instead, they wanted to express an intention about how being married will change the shape of their future. In that spirit, the promises they make today are a vision for the rest of their lives together.

Do you two promise to stay curious about each other, to keep discovering who the other is becoming, and to meet each other with wonder?
**We do.**

Do you promise that you will love each others’ people as your own, to create a family together, and to let your love encompass everyone around you?
**We do.**

Do you promise to help each other stay oriented toward the world, and to lean on your differences to move you both toward the vision you share?
**We do.**

Do you promise to give your love to each other freely and without conditions?
**We do.**

And finally, do you promise to build your home into a place where people are welcome, supported, and loved exactly as they are?
**We do.**
