#### where i was

When we met 5 years ago, I was in a season of re-defining myself and my future. Pretty newly polyamorous, identifying as non-binary, living alone for the first time in many years. I was very actively learning how to love with the depth I knew my heart to be capable, while also protecting myself. I wasn't looking for whatever *this* is, but, to be fair, I couldn't known to have. I had no idea *this* was possible.

#### what i noticed about you

The first thing i noticed about you was your smile, which radiated through your tinder pic. But what followed shortly after was your quick wit and conversation. It was hardly a day we had been talking, I think, before we were sending voice messages that were like 8 minutes long.. each message beget more threads to follow up on. We talked about love and relationships, and what the hell romance is (i'm still not sure I really know). I shared my vision for being exquisitely cared for by a community of people... where we each give what we can uniquely give and practice asking for and receiving love and care from each other. And we practiced that together! We were so candid with each other, so early. We both thoroughly believed (and still do) that there’s nothing sexy about making the other person guess what you want.

It really didn't take me long to realize that you were someone I wanted to build my life toward. I was terrified by how hard and fast I fell for you! It felt risky for it to feel so easy to be with you, and so obviously *right* to nuzzle into your shoulder. I wasn't sure whether I could ever build a home with someone as myself. But as each fear bubbled up, you met me with such tenderness. You held me as I felt each feeling and, over time, embodied the belief that I do not need to lose myself to be loved.

And so it went... we chose to build, together, in the space between us... healing and growing and learning about ourselves and each other along the way.

#### the project of family

And of course, the space between us was never just ours. Choosing you meant choosing Joe, and building a family that has room for all of us to be ourselves. Which, honestly, wasn't easy at first for me! You were made to be a dad and had just a few more years of parenting under your belt. But you never asked me to be anyone but myself. And as I've built more confidence in who I want to be as a parent, I'm only more rewarded by the opportunity you've given me to be a part of Joe's life.

#### conclusion

So yeah, 5 years ago, I had no idea this was possible, but little by little our future opened up. With each "flavor of the day" we wrote, each nightly ritual, each conversation, we have woven a rich tapestry of what's possible for our lives and our family. I feel so privileged to be loved by you and to get to build in the space between us.
