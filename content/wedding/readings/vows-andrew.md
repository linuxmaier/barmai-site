We fell in love fast. I remember it was really only a few months in before we started discussing what we needed to figure out before we moved in together, like we were talking about clearing away clutter so we could finally set our bags down. So it was an odd experience for me that it took us quite a while, and quite a lot of conversations, to figure out if, or really ‘why,’ we wanted to get married.

What we landed on, the ‘why,’ was that marriage is the word that best describes the project we’re trying to undertake together. The shared values about the lives we want to see ahead of us, the orientation we have towards living for love, the way we want to create home, how we value our resources and want to use them to experience life… We saw in each other a good partner to take each other into the world, and marriage was the word that fit when we wanted to thread so many strands of our lives together.

Alongside that foundation, I also was and am deeply in love with you. I’m reminded daily of how much I admire your curiosity, your vibrancy, and your connectedness to the world. I love your joy and your creativity. I grin at your competitiveness and learn from your ambition. I roll my eyes at how everywhere we go you bump into someone you know, but behind the eye roll I’m secretly amazed at how many lives you touch and how many people experience delight, as I do, when they see Hanna coming.

But it’s not just about that shared vision and deep admiration. There’s something about how I experience life with you that makes me feel so right to be standing here with you today. I don’t think I’ve put it to you this way before, but the thing that makes me want to celebrate and shout and dance about us is how beautiful it feels to be uncomfortable with you.

On our very earliest dates, it felt like we were practicing what it meant to be uncomfortable together. You were inviting me to do things I’d not had courage enough to try. You were being vulnerable and honest about what you needed and who you were, without knowing me well enough yet to be sure I’d get it. We were stepping bravely towards each other without knowing the path and its turns, risking stumbling into each other and hurting one another. And right away, in those first weeks of getting to know you, I realized with wonder that I wasn’t scared about making mistakes. Not because I didn’t think I would make them, but because you wanted me to take risks if it meant getting to know you better. To not shy away from the chance of misunderstanding but to embrace it as that first awkward moment of discovery.

I move through this world collecting knowledge like it’s armor, like I can wrap everything I know around me and use it to protect myself from the hurt of having been mistaken. With you, the state of being uncomfortable, of being uncertain, of ignorance is exciting, because it means I get to discover you more. And it’s such a precious gift to me, because when I am not protecting myself with knowledge and certainty, I get to be open to wonder and discovery. I get to be in the world. I get to be alive.

I want to be here, to get married, to have a wedding with you because you are so bravely, beautifully, powerfully good at being uncomfortable, and I want to do that with you.
